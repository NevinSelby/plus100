WHAT Plus100 IS (AND ISN'T)

Plus100 is a statistics tool. It estimates the probability of football outcomes from historical data and compares those probabilities against sportsbook prices. It is not a sportsbook, does not accept bets, and has no connection to any gambling operator.

PROBABILITIES ARE NOT PREDICTIONS OF CERTAINTY

Every number in this app is a probability, never a promise. A 60% chance means the outcome fails 4 times out of 10. Even the strongest "good bet" this app ever flags is expected to lose regularly. A positive edge only shows up as profit across many bets, and can take hundreds of bets to overcome normal luck. There is no such thing as a 100% win rate, here or anywhere.

MEASURED, HONEST ACCURACY

In testing on thousands of held-out matches, this model picks the correct result about as often as the sportsbooks themselves (roughly half the time), and its probabilities are calibrated: when it says 40%, that outcome happens about 40% of the time. That is the realistic ceiling of football prediction, for anyone.

WHERE THE EDGE REALLY COMES FROM

We tested it directly: against closing prices, nothing predicts more accurately than the betting market itself, this model included (validated on 4,227 unseen matches). So Plus100 anchors 80% to the market consensus and hunts its edges where they actually live: prices that differ between books, softer early prices, line moves that lock profits, and mispriced combos, not in out-predicting the bookmakers.

KNOWN LIMITS

The model cannot know lineups, late injuries, motivation, or weather unless you enter them (use the OUT buttons on scorer rows). Odds come from a third-party feed and can lag the sportsbook apps by minutes. Match data refreshes itself every 6 hours; the date at the top of the page shows exactly how current the ratings are.
